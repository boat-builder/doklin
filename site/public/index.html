The knowledge base your team writes and your agents read

Doklin keeps everything your company writes in one place, on your own domain. Write on your Mac, pick it up on your phone, share any page with a link, and let your agents read it and keep it up to date.

Download for macOS Connect an agent Free · open source · Apple silicon

01For your agents

Your agents read it, and keep it up to date.

Agents connect with one command, from any machine. They search and read the documents your team works in, as fresh as the last save, and write back what changed: a new number, a decision, a page for the meeting. There's no connector to set up and no copy to go stale.

Works with any agent that can run a command, on Linux and macOS.

They see what their person sees

An agent belongs to someone on the team and reaches exactly what they can, including their Private section and the restricted folders they're in.

You choose what they may do

Read, comment, edit, publish. Publishing stays off until you turn it on.

Their work comes back like a teammate's

Edits appear on your Mac and your phone, signed with the agent's name, and every version can be restored.

04Together

Your team and your agents, on the same page.

Invite your team with a code. They paste it into Doklin, on a Mac or a phone, and the workspace is theirs too. A question goes in a comment on the words it's about, and whoever knows the answer, person or agent, fixes the document.

Comments where the question is

Threads sit on the text itself, and anyone can reply: from a Mac, from a phone, or from an agent.

See who's working

Faces show who's online, and a page someone is editing opens read-only for everyone else until they're done.

Shared, Private, Restricted

Everyone gets the shared workspace and a Private section. A restricted folder is only for its people; to everyone else, it doesn't exist.

06Yours

Open source, free, and yours.

Free and open source

Download the app and use it on your own files. No account, no trial.

Your files

Everything is plain markdown, on your Macs and your phones. Export the whole workspace with its history as one archive that opens without Doklin.

Your cloud

The shared workspace runs on your own Cloudflare account, built for its free plan. There's no Doklin server in the middle.

Your agents

Doklin has no AI of its own. Use the agents you already trust.

07Set it up

Start with a markdown file. Grow into a knowledge base.

All it takes is a Mac, a Cloudflare account and the agent you already use. There's no Doklin account to make and no server of ours to sign up for.

Mac with Apple silicon Cloudflare account, free plan An agent that can run commands

    Install Doklin

    Download the app and open the notes on your Mac. It's a full editor from the first minute.

    Create the workspace

    Pick its address: a domain of your own, or a free workers.dev one. Doklin writes a prompt, and your agent sets it all up on your Cloudflare account.
