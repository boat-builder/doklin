01For your agents

Give your agents your company's context.

Agents connect with one command, from any machine. They search, read and write the same documents your team is working in, as fresh as the last save. There are no connectors to set up and no copy to keep in sync.

Works with Claude Code, Codex, or any agent that can run a command. Linux and macOS.

agent, on any machinedoklin
$ doklin search "pricing decision"
$ doklin read Strategy/pricing
$ doklin comment Strategy/pricing \
    --on "annual plan" -m "Numbers updated for Q1"
$ doklin publish Strategy/pricing

They see what their person sees

An agent belongs to someone on the team and reaches exactly what they can, including their Private section and the restricted folders they're in.

You choose what they may do

Read, comment, edit, publish. Publishing stays off until you turn it on.

Their work comes back like a teammate's

Edits appear in the app, signed with the agent's name, and every version can be restored.

02One document, two versions

Markdown for agents. A real page for people.

Agents work best in markdown, but people shouldn't have to read it raw. A Doklin document can hold both: the markdown your agents read and write, and a designed HTML page made from it for the people who read it. They're one document, with one name and one set of comments, and moving it moves both.

04For your company

One place for everything your company writes.

Invite your team with a code. They paste it into Doklin and the workspace is on their Mac. There's no folder to pick and no account to make.

Shared, Private, Restricted

Everyone gets the shared workspace and a Private section. Anyone can make a folder only its people can see; to everyone else, it doesn't exist.

See who's working

Faces show who's online, and a page someone is editing opens read-only for everyone else until they're done.

Publish to your own domain

A page, a folder or the whole workspace gets an address on your domain, and stopping publishing takes it down.

05Yours

Open source, free, and yours.

Free and open source

Download the app and use it on your own files. No account, no trial.

Your files

Everything is plain markdown on your Macs. Export the whole workspace with its history as one archive that opens without Doklin.

Your cloud

The shared workspace runs on your own Cloudflare account, built for its free plan. There's no Doklin server in the middle, and an agent sets it up from a prompt Doklin writes.

Your agents

Doklin has no AI of its own. Use the agents you already trust.
